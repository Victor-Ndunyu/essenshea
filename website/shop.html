How it works

Request-based ordering, simplified.

Browse products, build a list, submit - we confirm pricing and availability within 24 hours.

1. Browse & select

Open any product to review details, pricing, and availability. Add items you want to your request list.

2. Review & submit

Open the floating cart widget (top-right) to review your list, make changes, and submit your request.

3. Confirmation

We confirm pricing, availability, and schedule fulfilment. Made-to-order items are queued after your request.
